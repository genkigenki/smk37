Privacy

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub records technical access data such as your IP address when you open the page; see GitHub's privacy statement. This site itself sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads its fonts from this site, not from third parties.

The video on the start page is hosted on YouTube (Google). It is loaded from youtube-nocookie.com only when you press play; until then nothing is requested from YouTube. After that, Google's privacy policy applies.

The installer talks to your keyboard through Web MIDI, inside your browser. Nothing about you or your device is sent anywhere. The browser's local storage remembers which firmware an interrupted install was writing, so it can be finished, and which firmwares you liked; it never leaves your computer.

The firmware cards show install and like counts. For them the page sends anonymous requests to a counter on Cloudflare Workers: when an install finishes, and when you like or unlike a firmware. Only the totals per firmware are stored. For a short rate limit, a truncated one-way hash of the connection is kept for up to 5 minutes. No IP addresses and no cookies are stored. Cloudflare processes the requests as the host; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The idea box on the start page sends what you write there to the same counter: the firmware you picked, your message and, only if you fill it in, your contact. They are stored with the time of sending and are read only by the author; nothing else is stored, no IP address and no cookie. For the rate limit the same truncated one-way hash is used, kept for up to 5 minutes. The messages are deleted automatically after 90 days, or earlier if you ask at the address above. The contact is used only to answer you. The messages are forwarded to the author by email through Resend (Resend Inc., USA); see Resend's privacy policy.
