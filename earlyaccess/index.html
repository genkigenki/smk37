Vox37

new firmware · genkigenki · SMK-37 and FM-1

A vocoder: plug a battery-powered USB microphone into the keyboard, hold a chord and speak or sing. Confirmed working: DJI Mic Mini 2.

  • USB mic in, the keys play the voice
  • 4 modes, 8 memories, record & play a voice take
  • Also for the FM-1 (experimental)

Gen37

new firmware · genkigenki

A generative sequencer: eight tracks with Euclid, Turing, random, arp and step generators, chord follow and a harmoniser, on their own MIDI channels and an internal synth and drums.

  • 35 moods and styles in three banks: SPA, NIGHT DRIVE, HOUSE, BRAINDANCE, GLASSWORK …
  • EVOLVE: sections and slow drift, so a mood runs for minutes
  • MIDI out over USB and the TRS jack, clock in and out

Unofficial, experimental firmware, at your own risk. The FM-1 builds are untested on a unit.

Back to the M-Vave firmware

These firmwares keep your keyboard's model name, so M-Vave's own updater, M-UPGRADE, works directly (on the FM-1 too).

If your SMK-37 runs another firmware that M-UPGRADE refuses:

  1. Download the official firmware (.fwsc) for your keyboard:
    SMK-37 Pro: download V016
    SMK-37 Elite: download V016
    Other models: M-Vave's download page.
  2. Drop it here.
  3. Install.

Your keyboard's calibration and pairing are never touched. The firmware on the keyboard, and what it saved, is replaced. Keep the USB cable connected and this tab open until it says Done. Afterwards, M-UPGRADE should also work again if you need it.
