Unofficial, experimental firmware, at your own risk. The FM-1 builds are untested on a unit.

Back to the M-Vave firmware

These firmwares keep your keyboard's model name, so M-Vave's own updater, M-UPGRADE, works directly (on the FM-1 too).

If your SMK-37 runs another firmware that M-UPGRADE refuses:

  1. Download the official firmware (.fwsc) for your keyboard:
    SMK-37 Pro: download V016
    SMK-37 Elite: download V016
    Other models: M-Vave's download page.
  2. Drop it here.
  3. Install.

Your keyboard's calibration and pairing are never touched. The firmware on the keyboard, and what it saved, is replaced. Keep the USB cable connected and this tab open until it says Done. Afterwards, M-UPGRADE should also work again if you need it.
